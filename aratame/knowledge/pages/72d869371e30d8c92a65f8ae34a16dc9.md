# Membership suspension and identity

## Suspension scope
- Membership suspension blocks new checkout, renewal, locker assignment and locker pickup; it never blocks returning a borrowed item. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- These rules apply at desks and lockers. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- The service charter's identity, suspension and idempotency requirements apply to all exception paths. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA implication: for a suspended member, checkout, renewal, locker assignment and locker pickup are expected to be blocked at both desk and locker, while returning a borrowed item remains permitted. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]

## Returns and loan counts while suspended
- A return may be recorded for a suspended member and must reduce the active-loan count exactly once. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Locker reservation is not checkout; the active loan begins only after verified pickup. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- QA implication: recording a return for a suspended member is expected to reduce the active-loan count exactly once, not more than once. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]

## Already-assigned locker holds for suspended members
- The draft suggestion that suspended members may collect already-assigned locker holds was explicitly not approved for release 6. [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923]
- The deferred package keeps the two-renewal limit and person-bound codes; automatic third renewals and transferable family pickup codes were deferred and not approved for release 6. [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923]
- The decision states it is not an implementation request and directs demand investigation next quarter. [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923]
- QA implication: no exception permitting a suspended member to collect an already-assigned locker hold is approved; this non-approval is explicit rather than unspecified. [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923]

## Identity mismatch and custody
- Only a desk librarian may resolve an identity mismatch; an unattended terminal must leave custody unchanged. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- Every circulation or locker action records membership ID, item ID, branch, actor and outcome under one operation ID. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- A lost network acknowledgement must not create a second loan or release a second compartment. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- Audit retention is 90 days. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- The charter documents product authority only; it grants no permission to publish data or execute tests in any other system. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- QA implication: an unattended terminal encountering an identity mismatch is expected to leave custody unchanged; only a desk librarian may resolve the mismatch. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- QA implication: after a lost network acknowledgement for a circulation or locker action, no second loan and no second compartment release are expected. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- QA implication: each circulation or locker action is expected to carry membership ID, item ID, branch, actor and outcome under one operation ID, with audit retention of 90 days. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
