# Holds and locker assignment

## Hold queue and assignment order
- The first valid hold is assigned by request timestamp; equal timestamps use the recorded queue sequence, not member surname. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- An item with a waiting hold moves to sorting on return, not to available stock. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Branch transfers preserve the hold queue sequence and active-loan count. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Damaged returns enter inspection and do not satisfy the next hold until a librarian clears them. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Cancelled holds release their queue position and must be removed from any uncollected locker assignment before the item can be reassigned. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Renewal is not available when another member has an active hold. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- QA: A damaged return does not satisfy the next hold until a librarian clears it. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA: Cancelling a hold releases its queue position and requires removal from any uncollected locker assignment before reassignment. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]

## Locker reservation, pickup and limits
- Locker reservation is not checkout; the active loan begins only after verified pickup. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Staff inspect damage before placing a returned item on a pickup shelf or in a locker. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- A member at the five-loan limit cannot use locker pickup to evade the limit. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- The proposal to allow suspended members to collect already-assigned locker holds was explicitly not approved. [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923]
- QA: Locker pickup must not begin an active loan before verified pickup. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- QA: A member at the five-loan limit cannot evade the limit through locker pickup. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]

## Cancellation and locker code revocation
- A member may cancel an unassigned hold online. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Cancelling an assigned but uncollected hold requires the locker service to revoke its code; if revocation cannot be confirmed the item remains reserved for staff review. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA: An assigned but uncollected hold stays reserved for staff review when locker code revocation cannot be confirmed. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA: Online cancellation of an unassigned hold is permitted. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## Suspension, identity and idempotency constraints
- Membership suspension blocks new checkout, renewal, locker assignment and locker pickup; it never blocks returning a borrowed item. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- Every circulation or locker action records membership ID, item ID, branch, actor and outcome under one operation ID. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- A lost network acknowledgement must not create a second loan or release a second compartment. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- Only a desk librarian may resolve an identity mismatch; an unattended terminal must leave custody unchanged. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- The service charter's identity, suspension and idempotency requirements apply to all exception paths. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA: Suspension blocks locker assignment and locker pickup but never blocks returning a borrowed item. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- QA: A lost network acknowledgement must not create a second loan or release a second compartment. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- QA: Identity mismatch resolution is limited to a desk librarian; an unattended terminal leaves custody unchanged. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]

## Lost-item debt and locker codes
- An item reported lost retains its loan until staff confirms replacement or recovery; a locker code cannot clear that debt. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA: A locker code cannot clear the debt of an item reported lost; the loan remains until staff confirms replacement or recovery. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## Reconciliation and audit observations
- Daily reconciliation compares physical locker occupancy with pending assignments and sends discrepancies to staff, without fabricating a pickup or a return. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- A desk receipt lists each operation separately; batch return failure must not erase successful earlier returns. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- At branch closure, existing due dates use that branch's published calendar, not the device timezone. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Audit retention is 90 days. [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]
- QA: Daily reconciliation discrepancies go to staff; neither pickup nor return is fabricated. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- QA: Batch return failure must not erase successful earlier returns. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
