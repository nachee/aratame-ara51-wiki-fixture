# Lending (release 6 overview)

Release 6 lending treats the circulation desk, the locker bank and the daily exception/reconciliation process as one service. Three approved documents define it: the Juniper service charter (approved for release 6 on 2026-08-03 by the library director), the circulation desk operating agreement (release 6 behavior approved 2026-08-05 by the library director and the circulation lead) and the release 6 daily exception handbook (approved 2026-08-07 by the circulation lead under the director's release delegation) [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2] [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b] [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]. The charter describes itself as documenting product authority only and granting no permission to publish data or execute tests in any other system [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2].

## Capability map

- **Loans and renewals** — holds the five active-loan limit, the 21-calendar-day term due at branch closing on the due date, the on-site-only status of reference books, and the renewal rule: twice, each extending the existing due date by 14 days, but not when another member has an active hold [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b].
- **Holds and locker assignment** — covers hold ordering (first valid hold by request timestamp; equal timestamps by recorded queue sequence, not member surname), the routing of a returned item with a waiting hold to sorting rather than available stock, and locker reservation that only becomes a loan after verified pickup [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]; the handbook adds online cancellation of unassigned holds and the code-revocation requirement for assigned but uncollected holds [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04].
- **Membership suspension and identity** — suspension blocks new checkout, renewal, locker assignment and locker pickup but never blocks returning a borrowed item [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2]; a return may be recorded for a suspended member and must reduce the active-loan count exactly once [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]; only a desk librarian may resolve an identity mismatch, and an unattended terminal must leave custody unchanged [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2].
- **Returns and damage handling** — staff inspect damage before placing a returned item on a pickup shelf or in a locker [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]; damaged returns enter inspection and do not satisfy the next hold until a librarian clears them [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04].
- **Exception handling and audit** — desk receipts list each operation separately, batch return failure must not erase successful earlier returns, and daily reconciliation compares physical locker occupancy with pending assignments and sends discrepancies to staff without fabricating a pickup or a return [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]; every circulation or locker action records membership ID, item ID, branch, actor and outcome under one operation ID, with audit retention of 90 days [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2].
- **Deferred release decisions** — the proposal for automatic third renewals and transferable family pickup codes was deferred and not approved for release 6, keeping the two-renewal limit and person-bound codes [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923].

## Cross-cutting constraints

- The charter's identity, suspension and idempotency requirements apply to all exception paths, at desks and at lockers [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04] [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2].
- A lost network acknowledgement must not create a second loan or release a second compartment, and replaying a successful checkout or return returns its prior receipt without altering counts again [922e733e-29b5-4bcd-b8db-38d8acd6b2d2] [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b].
- Offline checkout is not supported: the unavailable-service message is shown and no loan is created [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b].
- At branch closure, existing due dates use that branch's published calendar, not the device timezone [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04].
- An item reported lost retains its loan until staff confirms replacement or recovery, and a locker code cannot clear that debt [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04].
- Branch transfers preserve the hold queue sequence and the active-loan count [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04].

## Notable interactions

- Locker reservation is deliberately not checkout: the active loan begins only after verified pickup, and a member at the five-loan limit cannot use locker pickup to evade that limit [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b].
- A member may cancel an unassigned hold online; cancelling an assigned but uncollected hold requires the locker service to revoke its code, and if revocation cannot be confirmed the item remains reserved for staff review [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]. Cancelled holds release their queue position and must be removed from any uncollected locker assignment before the item can be reassigned [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b].
- The deferred decision keeps the two-renewal limit, consistent with the operating agreement's two-renewal rule, and states that the draft's exception letting suspended members collect already-assigned locker holds was explicitly not approved [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923] [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b].
- The deferred decision is recorded as deferred and not approved, and as not an implementation request; demand is to be investigated next quarter [3b39abb6-997d-48d8-a11e-da7b2bf6e923].
