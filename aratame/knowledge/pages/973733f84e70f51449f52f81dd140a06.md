# Returns and damage handling

Release 6 behavior for returns is explicitly approved: the circulation desk operating agreement was approved on 2026-08-05, and the daily exception handbook entry on 2026-08-07 under a release delegation. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b] [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## Where a returned item goes

- On return, an item with a waiting hold moves to sorting, not available stock. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Damaged returns enter inspection and do not satisfy the next hold until a librarian clears them. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Staff inspect damage before placing a returned item on a pickup shelf or in a locker. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- An available ordinary book is loaned for 21 calendar days and is due at branch closing on the due date; at branch closure, existing due dates use that branch's published calendar, not the device timezone. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b] [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## Count, receipt and idempotency effects of recording a return

- A return may be recorded for a suspended member and must reduce the active-loan count exactly once. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Replaying a successful checkout or return operation returns its prior receipt and does not alter counts again. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- A desk receipt lists each operation separately; batch return failure must not erase successful earlier returns. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Branch transfers preserve the hold queue sequence and active-loan count. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- The service charter's identity, suspension and idempotency requirements apply to all exception paths. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## Items reported lost

- An item reported lost retains its loan until staff confirms replacement or recovery; a locker code cannot clear that debt. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## Returns interacting with holds, lockers and reconciliation

- Cancelled holds release their queue position and must be removed from any uncollected locker assignment before the item can be reassigned. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Cancelling an assigned but uncollected hold requires the locker service to revoke its code; if revocation cannot be confirmed the item remains reserved for staff review. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Locker reservation is not checkout; the active loan begins only after verified pickup. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Daily reconciliation compares physical locker occupancy with pending assignments and sends discrepancies to staff, without fabricating a pickup or a return. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]

## QA implications

- A returned item that has a waiting hold is expected to move to sorting rather than available stock. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- A damaged return is expected not to satisfy the next hold until a librarian clears it. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Damage inspection is expected before a returned item is placed on a pickup shelf or in a locker. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- A return recorded for a suspended member is expected to reduce the active-loan count exactly once. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- Replaying a return is expected to return the prior receipt and not alter counts again. [cd729138-dd10-4c76-b209-d2b44a4d104b]
- A batch return failure is expected to leave successful earlier returns intact, with each operation listed separately on the desk receipt. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- A reported-lost item is expected to retain its loan until staff confirm replacement or recovery, and a locker code is not expected to clear that debt. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Reconciliation is expected not to fabricate a pickup or a return. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
- Cancelling an assigned but uncollected hold is expected to leave the item reserved for staff review when locker-code revocation cannot be confirmed. [113cdf43-46ec-4e9b-ab84-bdb1da0ddb04]
